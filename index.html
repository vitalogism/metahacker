This has happened before.

The fault zone has produced 118 recorded ruptures since Startup News opened in February 2007. Most were small. The record below has one line per year, and the 2026 line runs out where the pen is.

Fault systems

Every event is assigned to one of five faults by the shape of its title. Each strip runs from 2007 to today.

  1. Bedrock

    Hacker News itself

    The original site and its source code. Everything else rests on it.

  2. But-For Fault

    Hacker News, but for X

    An HN-shaped site for another community: ideas, finance, designers, cars, gamedev, independent blogs.

  3. Mentioned-On Fault

    X, as mentioned on HN

    Mine the comments for books, videos, courses or papers, then rank them. The fault that produced the 2026 mainshock.

  4. Reskin Fault

    Hacker News, but as X

    The same stories in a new costume: newsprint, Teletext, Windows XP, The New York Times, TikTok. Also the converse, X but for Hacker News.

  5. Ouroboros Fault

    Hacker X, but for Hacker X

    Aggregators of the aggregators, and Hacker News submitted to Hacker News. This page sits on it.

Methods

Data
Posts, points and comment counts come from the HN Search API. A small script refreshes them every six hours and looks for new posts. Everything is in one JSON file.
Magnitude
MHN = 2.3 log10(points + 1), calibrated so that HackerTube lands near 7.9. A one-point post is M 0.7. Startup News predates the instruments; its magnitude is an estimate.
Faults
Events up to November 3, 2026 were classified and annotated by hand. Later events are classified by title patterns and marked unreviewed until someone reads them.
Forecast
Omori–Utsu rate n(t) = K / (t + c)p, fit by maximum likelihood over every post after the mainshock. Magnitudes follow Gutenberg–Richter with b estimated by Aki’s method. Probabilities assume a Poisson process, which is the most optimistic assumption on this page.
Corrections
Missed a clone, or found a fault in our faults? File an event report or open a pull request against the JSON.
